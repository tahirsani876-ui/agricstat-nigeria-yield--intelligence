
AGRICSTAT Nigeria Yield Intelligence

An agricultural statistics and yield intelligence platform for Nigeria.

AGRICSTAT aims to help farmers, researchers, and agricultural stakeholders understand crop yields, agricultural production, and market information.

The platform will support four languages:

- English
- Hausa
- Yoruba
- Igbo

Voice and Audio Features

AGRICSTAT will support voice-based services to help farmers who cannot read or write easily.

- Voice instructions in English, Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo.
- Text-to-speech so farmers can listen to agricultural information.
- Speech-to-text so farmers can ask questions by speaking.
- Voice guidance on crop yields, farming methods, weather, and market prices.
- Audio support for farmers with limited literacy.
- Simple voice navigation for farmers using mobile phones.
